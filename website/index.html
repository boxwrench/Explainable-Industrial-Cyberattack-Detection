02 / METHOD

Hybrid detection architecture

A 25-variable LSTM encodes recent behavior. Six audited scores describe process inconsistencies. A linear layer and sigmoid combine them for attack classification.

120 observations per window64 LSTM hidden units6 CORE symbolic scores

Original Figure 1 from the paper. Approximately 120 seconds per window. An elevated score identifies abnormal process behavior, not whether its cause is an attack, equipment fault, or sensor failure.

03 / EVALUATION

Held-out detection results

Comparison of four primary configurations on a fixed chronological TEST partition across five random seeds.

Statistical interpretation

CORE has the highest mean TEST MCC and F1 among these configurations. Full LSTM has the highest mean ROC-AUC. CORE versus Selected LSTM paired differences are not statistically significant across five seeds (p ≥ 0.0625). Error bars show standard deviation across seeds, not confidence intervals.

Research scope

This study evaluates one water-treatment testbed, SWaT. Absolute detection performance remains weak. The experiment does not establish deployment readiness or generalization to other plants. Parsimony here means fewer engineering relationships, rather than demonstrated faster inference.

Raw SWaT data and model checkpoints are not included in this site. Obtain the dataset independently through its provider's access process. All interactive values on this page are recorded research artifacts, not live model predictions.
